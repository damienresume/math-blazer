How fractions work

A fraction describes equal parts of a whole. It has two numbers. The bottom number, the denominator, tells you how many equal parts the whole is cut into. The top number, the numerator, tells you how many of those parts you are talking about.

A pizza is cut into 8 equal slices. If you take 3 slices, you have 3/8 of the pizza. The denominator is 8 and the numerator is 3.

Your turn

Answer each question on paper, then open the answer to check it.

Question 1

A pizza is cut into 8 equal slices. You eat 3 slices. What fraction of the pizza did you eat?

Show the answer

You ate 3/8 of the pizza. You took 3 slices out of 8 equal slices.

Question 2

What fraction of the same pizza is left?

Show the answer

5/8 is left. There were 8 slices, you ate 3, and 8 - 3 = 5.

Question 3

Which is more pizza, 1/2 of a pizza or 1/4 of the same pizza?

Show the answer

1/2 is more. When the whole is cut into fewer equal parts, each part is bigger. A half is bigger than a quarter.

Question 4

Are 2/4 and 1/2 the same amount of pizza?

Show the answer

Yes. If you cut a pizza into 4 equal slices, 2 slices cover the same area as half of the pizza. The fractions 2/4 and 1/2 are called equivalent fractions.
